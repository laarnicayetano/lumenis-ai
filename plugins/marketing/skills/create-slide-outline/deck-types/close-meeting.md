# Close meeting

A sales-facing deck for a quarter-close or management close meeting — audience is typically Sales VPs/leadership, purpose is showing marketing's contribution to revenue and rallying the final push. This is a different register from a QBR: less "here's our full performance report," more "here's the tactical case for the stretch run," even though the underlying data overlaps.

**Default shape** (adjust to how much of the quarter is left and what the content supports):

1. **Quarter-to-date recap** — performance so far (revenue, deal velocity, wins/challenges) to set the context for where things stand. Keep this tight; it's grounding, not the point of the deck.
2. **Marketing's contribution to pipeline** — media wins, leads generated, and where possible, attribution back to specific deals or accounts. This is the section that answers "what has marketing actually done for revenue."
3. **The tactical plan for what's left** — a granular near-term plan (e.g. next 30 days), broken into concrete actions with an owner and timeframe, not a restated strategy.
4. **Closing events/opportunities** — each remaining closing event (trade show, users meeting, webinar) gets its own slide with what it is, why it matters to close, and the specific ask of the room (e.g. attendance, follow-up).
5. **Forward look** — end on the next big driver on the horizon, positioned as pipeline for the *next* period, not a filler close.

If sales leadership needs a single closing summary slide (e.g. to end a longer deck), make it one page, simple and concise — headline number, 2-3 supporting proof points, one clear next step. Don't cram detail into it; that's what the preceding slides are for.

Tone: direct and revenue-anchored throughout — every section should trace back to "what does this mean for pipeline/closing," per [core:audience-archetypes](../../../../core/skills/audience-archetypes/SKILL.md)'s Executive Team / Sales persona guidance. Output format is the same plain-markdown deck as any other deck — see the parent [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md).
