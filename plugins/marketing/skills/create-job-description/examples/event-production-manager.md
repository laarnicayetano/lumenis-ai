EVENT PRODUCTION MANAGER

POSITION OVERVIEW

The Event Production Manager is a strategic role critical to delivering exceptional field activation experiences that drive customer acquisition and sales success. Reporting directly to the VP of Marketing, this role demands a dynamic executioner who can simultaneously manage multiple complex projects while maintaining creative vision and operational excellence in the competitive energy-based device industry.

This is a senior-level position with significant responsibilities and accountability.

Travel requirement: Up to 75% travel, primarily across the US

CRITICAL EXPECTATIONS & RESPONSIBILITIES

TIER 1 - ESSENTIAL (Must Excel)

Field Activation Leadership
• Lead end-to-end production and execution of 25+ major field activation initiatives annually, including national tradeshows, regional symposiums, market development dinners, sales accelerator workshops, and other field activation support
• Oversee all production elements: venue selection, environment design, logistics coordination, vendor management, internal team logistics, and on-site operations
• Develop and manage comprehensive field activation budgets ranging from $50K-$500K+ per initiative, with full P&L accountability and budget management oversight
• Simultaneously manage 25+ complex production projects in various phases with competing deadlines

Strategic Planning & Execution
• Develop and execute long-term strategic planning with 6 months to 1 year advance timelines for optimal vendor selection, cost management, and execution quality
• Create executive-level field activation plans that demonstrate how events will support company objectives
• Present activation concepts and strategic rationale to leadership and stakeholders
• Articulate detailed execution methodologies and produce actionable timelines for cross-functional team members to complete their marketing functions

Operations & Coordination
• Serve as central hub for internal logistics coordination across service, clinical, and sales teams
• Collaborate with marketing leadership to create events that support sales objectives and customer engagement
• Coordinate lead capture systems and work with marketing operations team to upload and distribute leads to the sales team
• Report to marketing leadership the necessary data for them to run their performance metrics

TIER 2 - PROFICIENT (Expected Standards)

Creative Production & Technology
• Oversee environment design, fabrication, and visual presentation elements
• Manage audio/visual requirements, lighting design, and technical production needs
• Leverage event production technologies and technical integration solutions
• Ensure brand consistency and professional presentation across all activation touchpoints

Vendor & Financial Management
• Negotiate contracts and manage vendor relationships for optimal cost and quality outcomes
• Handle invoice processing, expense management, and financial reconciliation
• Coordinate travel, accommodations, and logistics for speakers and team members
• Implement new production tools and processes for enhanced efficiency

TIER 3 - ADVANTAGEOUS (Nice to Have)

Team Development & Innovation
• Provide production training and operational guidance to internal teams
• Support cross-departmental collaboration for seamless event execution
• Drive creative production solutions for unique event challenges and requirements
• Explore innovative production techniques and emerging event technologies

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

Non-Negotiable Requirements
• Bachelor's degree (MBA or advanced certification strongly preferred)
• Minimum 5-7 years of event production experience with demonstrated progression of responsibilities
• Minimum 3 years in healthcare, energy-based device industry, or high-end corporate event production
• Proven track record managing production budgets exceeding $2M annually across multiple projects
• Expert knowledge of event fabrication, A/V systems, and technical production requirements

Essential Skills & Attributes
• Production leadership experience managing teams and complex vendor relationships
• Creative and innovative mindset with ability to develop unique activation concepts and experiential solutions
• Exceptional strategic planning and execution abilities with demonstrated proactive approach to long-term project development (6 months to 1 year advance planning)
• Excellent communication skills with ability to present concepts, articulate execution methodologies, and coordinate across multiple stakeholders
• Strong technical knowledge of event production elements and tradeshow environments (A/V, lighting, fabrication, logistics)
• Self-motivated with ability to work independently while managing high-stakes deadlines
• Demonstrated ability to remain agile and fluid while maintaining flawless execution standards and solving production challenges in real-time
• "Can-do" attitude with solution-oriented approach to production obstacles and excellent communication skills

Preferred Qualifications
• Experience with production management software, CAD programs, and technical design tools
• Prior event production agency or experiential marketing company experience
• Knowledge of energy-based device markets and industry activation environments
• Certifications in event production, project management (PMP), or related technical fields
• Experience with international event production and logistics

SUCCESS METRICS & ACCOUNTABILITY

This role will be measured on:
• Event production quality and execution excellence (zero critical failures, 95%+ stakeholder satisfaction, on-time delivery rate >98%)
• Budget management and cost optimization (variance targets within 5%, vendor cost improvements, annual budget adherence)
• Production performance and delivery (25+ activations executed annually, timeline accuracy >95%)
• Strategic planning effectiveness (6 months to 1 year advance planning completion rate, cross-functional timeline delivery accuracy)
• Technical production innovation and process improvements that enhance efficiency or reduce costs
• Communication and coordination excellence (actionable timeline delivery, stakeholder feedback scores)

COMPENSATION PHILOSOPHY

This position represents a significant career advancement opportunity with compensation reflecting the strategic importance and accountability level required. The successful candidate will demonstrate immediate impact and growth potential.

The responsibilities outlined above represent the core functions of this role. Additional duties may be assigned to support organizational objectives and operational needs.
