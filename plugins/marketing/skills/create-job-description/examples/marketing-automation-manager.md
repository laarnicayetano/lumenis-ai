# Marketing Automation Manager

## Position Overview

The Marketing Automation Manager is responsible for developing, implementing, and optimizing B2B marketing campaigns using HubSpot and Salesforce across US territories. This role focuses on creating sophisticated automation workflows that align marketing activities with US field sales teams, nurture enterprise leads through complex buying cycles, and contribute to B2B revenue growth in the American market. The position requires deep expertise in both HubSpot and Salesforce integration to ensure seamless marketing-to-sales handoff across different US regions and territories.

## Key Responsibilities

### Daily Responsibilities & Activities

- Monitor and optimize active B2B marketing campaigns across US regions
- Manage lead distribution across five major US territories (NE, SE, MW, SW, W)
- Review and maintain HubSpot-Salesforce sync status for US operations
- Update lead scoring and routing rules based on regional sales team feedback
- Manage territory-specific ABM workflows and sales notifications
- Coordinate with regional sales directors on territory-specific campaign needs
- Maintain state and region-based lead assignment rules
- Support US field sales teams with campaign data and analytics
- Ensure proper time zone management for automated communications
- Monitor compliance with state-specific marketing regulations
- Optimize workflows based on regional market differences
- Coordinate with US-based sales enablement team on content distribution

### Strategy & Planning

- Develop and execute the marketing automation strategy aligned with overall marketing and business objectives
- Create and maintain a marketing automation calendar coordinating email, social media, and other digital campaigns
- Design and implement lead scoring models to qualify prospects and optimize the sales handoff process
- Establish KPIs and reporting frameworks to measure campaign effectiveness and ROI
- Plan and document automation workflows for different customer segments and journey stages
- Develop testing strategies for continuous optimization of automated campaigns
- Create documentation for standard operating procedures and best practices

### Technical Implementation

- Manage and optimize HubSpot marketing automation platform, ensuring seamless integration with Salesforce for US operations
- Configure and maintain HubSpot-Salesforce sync settings with US-specific field mappings and workflow triggers
- Build and maintain region-specific nurture workflows for Northeast, Southeast, Midwest, Southwest, and Western territories
- Create and manage US territory-based lead routing rules aligned with regional field sales structure
- Implement tracking parameters for US buying journey stages and account engagement scoring
- Develop custom fields and properties in both HubSpot and Salesforce for US market segmentation
- Configure state and region-based lead assignment rules and routing logic
- Maintain compliance with US data privacy regulations and state-specific requirements
- Set up automated notifications based on US time zones and regional sales team schedules

### Campaign Management

- Create and execute multi-touch marketing campaigns across various channels
- Develop personalized content strategies based on customer segments and behaviors
- A/B test campaign elements to optimize performance
- Monitor campaign performance and make data-driven adjustments
- Collaborate with content team to ensure alignment with automation workflows

### Analytics & Reporting

- Generate regular reports on key marketing metrics and campaign performance
- Analyze customer journey data to identify optimization opportunities
- Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders
- Create and maintain dashboards for real-time campaign monitoring

### Cross-functional Collaboration

- Partner with sales team to optimize lead handling and scoring processes
- Work with content creators to develop assets for automated campaigns
- Coordinate with web development team on landing pages and forms
- Train team members on marketing automation best practices
