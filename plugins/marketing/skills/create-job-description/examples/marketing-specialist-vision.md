# Marketing Specialist

Lumenis Vision

## Overview

The Marketing Specialist for Lumenis Vision is responsible for developing and executing marketing strategies that drive growth across both disruptive and legacy product lines. This cross-functional role spans the full marketing mix — from industry tradeshows and sponsored events to digital campaigns and brand communications.

The ideal candidate will partner closely with marketing leadership to prioritize high-impact activities and deliver measurable results tied to clear revenue goals. This role requires a collaborative mindset, strong organizational skills, and the ability to work effectively with global colleagues and field representatives.

## Key Responsibilities

- Drive engagement through strategic participation in high-impact industry tradeshows, virtual events, webinars, and other relevant activities — identifying key opportunities and representing the brand effectively.
- Develop marketing materials, presentations, and communications in support of product launches, campaigns, sales meetings, tradeshows, and events.
- Strategize and execute integrated campaigns leveraging both traditional (print advertising, collateral, direct mail) and digital (social media, email, digital advertising, websites) marketing channels.
- Own the media plan, editorial calendar, and internal and external communications within the area of responsibility.
- Collaborate with US and Business Unit Marketing and MarCom teams to help shape overarching marketing strategies across websites, e-marketing tools, digital advertising, social media, and other digital tactics.
- Develop and manage promotional programs to generate leads at key shows and events; partner with external vendors to ensure programs run on time, within scope, and on budget.
- Continuously monitor program performance, including ROI analytics, sales objectives, budget tracking, and advertising and promotion reporting.
- Assess existing marketing materials — revising literature as needed, archiving outdated assets, and maximizing digital-first approaches wherever possible.
- Manage administrative responsibilities including Salesforce updates, business plans, expense reporting, budget forecasting, and weekly activity reports.

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Advertising, Communications, Public Relations, or a related field.
- 2–3+ years of experience in marketing with a strong focus on events and communications.
- Proficiency in MS Office and marketing tools including Adobe Creative Suite, Salesforce, HubSpot, Google Ads, and web analytics platforms.
- Demonstrated experience in campaign design and execution.
- Experience with data analytics and dashboard creation.
- Highly creative and strategic thinker with the ability to identify target audiences and develop campaigns that engage, inform, and motivate.
- Exceptional organizational and project management skills; ability to thrive in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.
- Healthcare or medical device industry experience is a strong plus.
- Willingness and ability to travel approximately 20–25% of the time to attend and support relevant activations.

If you're a motivated marketing professional with a passion for driving growth in a dynamic, collaborative environment, we'd love to hear from you.
