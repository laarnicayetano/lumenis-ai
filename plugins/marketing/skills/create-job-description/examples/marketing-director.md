# Marketing Director

## Position Overview

The Marketing Director is responsible for developing, implementing, and executing strategic marketing initiatives across the organization. This senior leadership role combines creative vision with analytical thinking to drive brand awareness, lead generation, and revenue growth while managing and mentoring a high-performing marketing team.

## Core Responsibilities

### Strategic Leadership

- Develop and execute comprehensive marketing strategies aligned with company objectives and growth targets
- Lead the creation and management of annual marketing budgets and resource allocation
- Establish and track key performance indicators (KPIs) to measure and optimize marketing effectiveness
- Collaborate with C-level executives to align marketing initiatives with overall business strategy

### Team Management

- Lead, mentor, and develop a team of marketing professionals across various specialties
- Foster a culture of creativity, innovation, and data-driven decision-making
- Conduct regular performance reviews and provide growth opportunities for team members
- Manage relationships with external agencies and vendors

### Marketing Operations

- Oversee all marketing channels including digital, content, social media, events, and traditional marketing
- Drive brand strategy and ensure consistent messaging across all platforms and materials
- Direct market research initiatives to identify trends, opportunities, and competitive insights
- Optimize marketing automation and lead generation systems

### Cross-functional Leadership

- Partner with Sales to develop and execute strategies that drive revenue growth
- Collaborate with Product Development to ensure effective go-to-market strategies
- Work with Customer Success to leverage customer insights and testimonials
- Coordinate with Finance to track ROI and optimize marketing spend

## Required Qualifications

### Education

- Bachelor's degree in Marketing, Business Administration, or related field
- Master's degree preferred (MBA or similar)

### Experience

- 10+ years of progressive marketing experience
- 5+ years in a senior marketing leadership role
- Proven track record of developing and executing successful marketing strategies
- Experience managing and growing marketing teams of 5+ people
- Demonstrated success in B2B and/or B2C marketing environments

### Skills & Competencies

- Expert knowledge of modern marketing principles and best practices
- Strong analytical skills with experience in data-driven decision making
- Excellence in strategic planning and execution
- Advanced project management and organizational abilities
- Outstanding written and verbal communication skills
- Proficiency in marketing analytics tools and CRM systems

### Leadership Qualities

- Strong emotional intelligence and team-building capabilities
- Excellent problem-solving and decision-making abilities
- Adaptability and comfort with change
- Strategic thinking with attention to detail
- Ability to influence and collaborate across departments

## Working Conditions

- Full-time position based at corporate headquarters
- Occasional travel required (20-25%)
- Flexible work arrangements available
- Benefits include health insurance, 401(k), and professional development allowance

## Career Advancement Criteria

To be considered for promotion to this role, candidates should demonstrate:

- Consistent achievement of marketing objectives and KPIs
- Successful management of substantial marketing budgets
- Track record of developing and mentoring team members
- Strategic contributions to company growth
- Strong relationships across the organization
- Industry recognition or thought leadership

## Reporting Structure

- Reports to: Chief Marketing Officer or Chief Executive Officer
- Direct Reports: Marketing Managers, Digital Marketing Lead, Content Strategy Lead, Marketing Operations Manager

## Success Metrics

Performance will be evaluated based on:

- Achievement of marketing-attributed revenue targets
- Brand awareness and market position improvements
- Team development and retention
- Marketing ROI and budget management
- Innovation in marketing strategies and execution
- Cross-functional collaboration effectiveness

## Salary Range & Benefits

- Competitive base salary commensurate with experience
- Performance-based bonus structure
- Equity compensation package
- Comprehensive benefits package
- Professional development allowance
- Executive wellness program
