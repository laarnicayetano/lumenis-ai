## Generic reminders (placeholder only)

- **No unapproved medical or clinical claims.** Don't state or imply outcomes, safety, or efficacy beyond what's in officially approved marketing materials for the product/device referenced.
- **Before/after images**: only use images explicitly cleared for marketing use, with any required consent on file. Never use a before/after that hasn't been approved for external posting.
- **Patient privacy**: no identifiable patient information, images, or stories without documented consent and approval.
- **Regulatory language**: device/procedure claims may be region- or market-specific (e.g. cleared/approved indications differ by country). Don't assume a claim valid in one market is valid in another.
- **Competitor mentions**: avoid direct comparisons or disparagement of named competitors.
- **Personal opinion vs. company claim**: if a rep is posting in a personal capacity, encourage clear framing ("in my experience...") rather than stating company claims as their own.
- **Pricing and offers**: don't include pricing, discounts, or promotional offers unless explicitly approved for the campaign.
- **Sourcing**: any statistic, study reference, or data point should be traceable to an approved source — don't include a number without knowing where it came from.
