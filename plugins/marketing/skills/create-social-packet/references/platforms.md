# Platform specs — LinkedIn & Instagram

Reference for drafting copy and sizing media. Only these two platforms are in scope for this skill.

## LinkedIn

- **Purpose here**: B2B credibility, clinician/practice-owner audience, thought leadership and product education. This is where a sales rep's post reads as professional, not promotional.
- **Caption**: Hook in the first 1–2 lines — LinkedIn truncates behind "…see more" fast, so the reason to keep reading has to land before the cut. Plain text reads better than a wall of hashtags; 3–5 relevant hashtags at the end is plenty.
- **Length**: Short-to-medium works best for a rep repost (3–8 sentences or a tight bullet list). Save long-form storytelling for corporate/brand accounts, not a rep's individual packet.
- **Tone**: Professional, informative, first-person credible ("Here's what I saw at..." / "Why practices are adopting..."). Avoid hard-sell language — LinkedIn audiences respond to insight and social proof over a pitch.
- **Images**: 1200×627 px (single image, landscape) is the safe default for link-preview-style posts; 1080×1080 px (square) for a native image post or carousel. Carousels (PDF-style multi-page documents) perform well for education but are out of scope for this skill's generated output — flag as an option in the README, not something this skill assembles.
- **Video**: 16:9 landscape or 1:1 square, under 10 minutes (best under 2 for feed attention).
- **Link placement**: Put the tracked link in a comment or the first line if click-through matters — native LinkedIn posts with a link embedded in the body get deprioritized in reach. Note this tradeoff in the post copy doc rather than silently choosing one.

## Instagram

- **Purpose here**: Visual proof — before/after energy, lifestyle/aesthetic appeal, patient-facing feel even though the rep is the one posting. Reused mainly as a feed post or Story by the rep or their practice contact.
- **Caption**: First 125 characters show before "more" — front-load the hook. Emoji are fine and expected here in a way they aren't on LinkedIn. Hashtags: 5–15 relevant ones, placed at the end of the caption or in the first comment.
- **Tone**: Warmer, more visual-first, can be shorter than LinkedIn — the image or video is doing most of the work, the caption supports it.
- **Images**: Feed post — 1080×1080 px (square) or 1080×1350 px (portrait, preferred for feed real estate). Story — 1080×1920 px (9:16).
- **Video (Reels)**: 1080×1920 px (9:16), under 90 seconds, ideally under 30 for feed attention. Reels get meaningfully more reach than a static image post — call this out in the README if a video asset exists for the campaign.
- **Alt text**: Instagram supports it; include a plain-language description of the image for accessibility if the source material supports writing one.

## Cross-platform notes for this skill

- Never reuse the exact same caption verbatim across both platforms — same core message, different framing per the tone notes above.
- Always write platform-specific hashtags — LinkedIn and Instagram hashtag conventions and audiences differ.
- Any medical/clinical claim, before/after image, or outcome statement follows [../references/compliance-reminders.md](compliance-reminders.md) — flag it rather than writing around it.
