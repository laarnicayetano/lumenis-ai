# Splendor X

Splendor X is a laser hair removal platform (an upgrade to the original system) using Blend X™ technology — a dual-wavelength blend of 755 nm Alexandrite and 1064 nm Nd:YAG.

## Target audience
- Patients seeking laser hair removal, primarily.
- Secondary use: patients with vascular or pigmented lesions.
