## Who it serves & positioning

Ophthalmologists, optometrists, and eye-care practices seeking clinical
equipment for dry eye disease, glaucoma, and retinal conditions, plus a path
to expand practice revenue through in-office procedures. Lumenis positions
itself as a 60+ year pioneer in ophthalmic innovation — credited with the
first SLT laser, the first argon laser photocoagulator, the first IPL
technology for dry eye management, and the first digital dual-path SLT-YAG
platform.

Tagline: **"The Beauty of Eye Care."**

Vision maintains localized sites for Australia, France, Germany, Italy,
Japan, the UK, China, and Russia, alongside the US site — treat this as
evidence of a real multi-market footprint, not a specific country count (the
"88 countries" figure quoted in `core:brand-context`/`core:aesthetics` is a
company- or Aesthetics-level stat; don't reuse it as a Vision-specific claim
without a Vision-specific source).

## Technology platforms

- **IPL (Optimal Pulse Technology / OPT™)** — the same core platform used on
  the Aesthetics side, applied here to dry eye disease caused by meibomian
  gland dysfunction (MGD). A xenon flash lamp generating 400-1200nm
  wavelengths reduces ocular inflammation, stimulates meibomian gland cells
  to restore oil production, treats abnormal blood vessels, and has an
  antimicrobial effect against Demodex mites. Standard protocol: 4 sessions,
  10-15 minutes each, spaced 2-4 weeks apart, with maintenance every 6-12
  months — many patients report improvement after 2-3 sessions.
- **Dynamic Muscle Stimulation (DMSt™)** — electrical impulses that tone
  periorbital muscles, addressing lower lid laxity and impaired blinking
  (OptiLIFT).
- **Dual-frequency RF** — 1MHz for deep-layer heat penetration and 4MHz for
  superficial-layer collagen production; output up to 250W across
  470kHz/1MHz/2MHz/4MHz/6MHz, monopolar electrodes from 10mm to 100mm
  diameter (OptiPLUS).
- **SLT (Selective Laser Trabeculoplasty) + YAG laser** — a dual-path
  platform for glaucoma. SLT uses targeted photothermolysis on
  melanin-containing cells to trigger the eye's natural outflow mechanism
  and lower intraocular pressure, without the systemic side effects or
  compliance issues of medication; it's non-destructive, outpatient, and
  Medicare/insurance-covered in the US. YAG addresses posterior capsule
  opacification (PCO) after cataract surgery. Digital Duet/Trio are
  described as the current generation; Selecta Duet/Trio as an earlier
  product family.
- **Photocoagulation** — laser-generated heat cauterizes blood vessels or
  diseased retinal tissue to seal leaks, destroy pathology, or anchor
  detached retinas. Includes green/yellow/red multi-wavelength systems,
  SmartPulse™ sub-threshold technology, and pattern-scanning laser delivery.

## Product portfolio

- **Dry eye:** OptiLIGHT (IPL; positioned as the first and only IPL
  FDA-cleared for DED/MGD management in the US), OptiPLUS (dual-frequency
  RF; FDA-cleared; complements OptiLIGHT and also targets facial skin
  rejuvenation as an adjunct), Optima IPL (an IPL platform distinct from
  OptiLIGHT in Lumenis's current materials — confirm positioning/differences
  before using both names in the same piece), OPT Shields (protective
  accessory used during IPL treatment), Antares (corneal topographer for
  dry eye diagnostics — a diagnostic instrument, not a treatment device).
- **Lower eyelid laxity:** OptiLIFT (DMSt; non-surgical, no downtime,
  addresses impaired blinking and evaporative dry eye alongside eyelid
  laxity — see `core:product-optilift` for full detail).
- **Glaucoma (SLT/YAG):** Digital Duet ("the first and only all-in-one
  digital platform for Glaucoma and PCO therapies"), Digital Trio (3-in-1,
  glaucoma + retina care), Smart Selecta Duet (SLT + YAG), Smart Selecta
  Trio (SLT + YAG + Smart532).
- **Retina (photocoagulators):** Smart532™ (green, SmartPulse sub-threshold
  technology), Array™ LaserLink™ (pattern scanning), Vision One
  (multi-wavelength: green 532nm, yellow 577nm, red 659nm), Novus Spectra
  (dual-port green, for OR and clinic), LIOs (laser indirect
  ophthalmoscopes), LumeProbe™ (endo-photocoagulation laser probes).
- **Diagnostic/surgical accessories:** Slit lamps, surgical probes.
