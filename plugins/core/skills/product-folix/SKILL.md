# FoLix

FoLix is a hair regrowth device: a 1565 nm class 4 non-ablative fractional laser, FDA cleared for improving the appearance of scalp hair in adult males and females with Fitzpatrick skin types I–IV who are seeking treatment for hair loss.

## Target audience
- Adult men and women with Fitzpatrick skin types I–IV seeking hair loss treatment, positioned for early-to-moderate stage thinning.
- Market context: 2 in 3 men face hair loss by age 35; 1 in 2 women face hair loss by age 50. (Lumenis also cites a broader stat: 85%+ of men and 50%+ of women experience hair loss, and 80M+ people affected in the US.)

## Talking points
- Headline claim: first and only FDA-cleared non-ablative fractional laser for hair loss treatment. FDA cleared June 5, 2024.
- No topical anesthetic, no chemicals, no needles, no surgery, no downtime after treatment.
- Quick ~30 minute procedure.
- No consumable.
- MOA: laser promotes blood circulation and growth factors to stimulate the hair follicle.
- Awards: NewBeauty "Best Laser Treatment for Hair Loss" (2025 and 2026), MedTech Breakthrough Award 2025 (Best New Dermatology Technology Solution), Medical Device Network Excellence Award 2025 (Hair Loss Technology Innovation).

## Features
- Laser specs: 1565 nm class 4 non-ablative fractional laser.
- FLX™ Technology: ensures optimal irradiance and area coverage.
- FoliCool™: patient comfort and safety.
- Clinical results: 10–18% increase in number of hairs per cm², and 13–16% additional follicular units per cm².
- Treatment protocol: recommended 3 to 6 treatments spaced 4 weeks apart; maintenance treatments recommended 2–3 times a year. (Note: most current Lumenis marketing pages instead say "4 to 6 monthly sessions" — worth confirming which protocol description is current before next use.)
- Available markets: US, Canada — Lumenis also states broader presence across the Middle East and Asia-Pacific within FoLix's first year; confirm specific countries before citing.

## Competitors
- Devices: LaserMD, Alma TED.
- Orals: Nutrafol.
- Surgery: hair transplant.
