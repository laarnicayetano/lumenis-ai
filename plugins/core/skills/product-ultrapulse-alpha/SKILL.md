# Ultrapulse Alpha

Ultrapulse Alpha is Lumenis's next-generation CO2 laser device, launched in 2023. Lumenis commercialized the first CO2 laser device (ULTRAPulse) in 1990, so Ultrapulse Alpha continues a 60-year device lineage.

## Target audience
- Hospitals and clinical practices, per FDA-approved indications.

## Talking points
- Next generation of a category Lumenis pioneered — the original ULTRAPulse CO2 laser (1990).
- Efficient, effective, uniform delivery of energy.
- Ablation + coagulation MOA: 10,600 nm CO2, targeting water as the chromophore.
- WTF principle — wavelength, time, and fluence — governs energy delivery during a cell's photothermal relaxation time while avoiding skin damage.

## Features
- MOA: ablation + coagulation; 10,600 nm CO2; high absorption of water (the target chromophore).
- Treatment applicators:
  - UltraScan — full and fractionated superficial resurfacing.
  - TrueSpot — ideal for rhinophyma treatment.
  - DeepFX Microscanner — deep fractional resurfacing, up to 4 mm for a single pulse.
  - Incision HP — incision/excision tool; used in blepharoplasty; ideal for treating acne scars.
