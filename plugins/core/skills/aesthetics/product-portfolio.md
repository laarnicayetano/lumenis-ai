## Category notes

- **Facial muscle stimulation** — triLift combines three technologies (DMSt, RF, and manual application) for a non-invasive, face-lift-like result; positioned as "a new category in facial stimulation aesthetics," not a competitor to injectables so much as an alternative to them.
- **Hair removal** — SPLENDOR X and LightSheer QUATTRO cover the category from two angles: SPLENDOR X pairs Alexandrite and Nd:YAG in one system (BLEND X technology) with high fluence, a large spot size, and a built-in smoke evacuator for speed and coverage; LightSheer QUATTRO's dual diode wavelengths (805nm/1060nm) extend permanent reduction claims across all skin types, including darker skin tones where some technologies underperform.
- **Hair loss** — FoLix is the newest addition to the portfolio, with recurring award recognition and a Bosley partnership expanding market access beyond Lumenis's traditional practice-equipment channel into a consumer-facing hair-restoration brand (the Bosley detail comes from resource-hub coverage, not the FoLix product page itself — worth a source check before treating it as settled positioning).
- **Skin (resurfacing/tightening)** — the largest and most technologically varied category: ablative CO2 (ULTRApulse Alpha for single-treatment deep resurfacing; AcuPulse for acne scars, pigmentation, and general resurfacing) for the deepest/highest-downtime end; IPL-based systems (Stellar M22, with 30+ IPL/laser treatment applications across resurfacing, rejuvenation, and lesion removal; M22 as the base multi-application platform Stellar M22 builds on) for broader multi-application coverage; and RF microneedling (Legend Pro/Legend Pro+, using TriPollar® RF) for collagen remodeling with less downtime. Positioned as one platform family covering the full severity/downtime spectrum rather than one device trying to do everything.
- **Body contouring** — NuEra Tight is a single-product category for now, using FocalRF to address fat reduction, skin tightening, cellulite, and wrinkle reduction in one device rather than requiring separate systems for each.
- **Women's health** — a dedicated, distinct category (not a repurposed skin product): FemTouch targets vaginal health via epithelium remodeling; AcuPulseW combines that with general ablative resurfacing for combined aesthetic and women's-health use.

## The throughline

Lumenis is one of the only companies with real depth across every modality — IPL, CO2, diode, Nd:YAG, and RF microneedling. Most competitors own one lane well (see [competitors.md](competitors.md) — InMode on RF, BTL on body contouring, Cynosure Lutronic on a single ambassador-led launch). Lumenis's pitch across categories isn't "our device in this modality is best," it's "we're not tied to one modality" — that's the core positioning fact everything else in this portfolio builds on, and it's worth reinforcing whenever a request spans more than one category.
