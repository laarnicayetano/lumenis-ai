# Aesthetics — Competitors

Ten-plus companies compete in this space, but don't try to learn all of them with equal depth. Three matter most right now; everyone else gets a one-paragraph mental model, not a deep study.

## Tier-1 competitors

**InMode** — the biggest direct threat on RF/body contouring. Revenue is under pressure (roughly $500M → $370M, 2023–2025) from high financing costs on capital equipment, but the company is still highly profitable and cash-rich. Their real strength isn't the device — it's that every practice that buys Morpheus8 gets handed ready-to-post content, so patients see it everywhere organically (Kim Kardashian posting about it unprompted is the extreme version of this working).

**BTL Aesthetics** — Emsculpt is the category-definer in body contouring, built almost entirely on clinical proof-point volume (30+ studies, 1M+ treatments, 25B+ media impressions) rather than paid social.

**Cynosure Lutronic (XERF)** — the most instructive case study in the category. Launched in Korea, not the US; built ambassador-led consumer demand in Asia-Pacific before clinical rollout; adoption tripled in pace once it hit critical mass. This is the playbook for "build patient pull before provider push."

## Everyone else (one-paragraph mental model each)

- **Candela / Sciton / Solta (Merz) / Cutera / Rohrer** — legacy or value-focused, digitally quiet. A real gap in their go-to-market, not just missing data on our side.
- **Alma** — wins beauty-press credibility through design awards, not clinical volume.
- **Sofwave** — small but sharp: the only competitor running a real SEO/content strategy tied to trending searches (GLP-1 body changes, male aesthetics).
