# OptiLIGHT claims reference

A sourced audit of clinical, regulatory and epidemiological claims/statistics used in current OptiLIGHT marketing materials, with citations and a per-claim review status.

**Read before quoting a figure externally.** This is a record of what current materials say and what their own footnotes cite — it is **not** pre-cleared copy and not a substitute for regulatory/medical/legal review of new copy. The large majority of rows below (13 of 15) are marked `needs review`: the cited source couldn't be independently confirmed, the number doesn't clearly reconcile with the source, or two current materials give different numbers for the same claim — including a couple of likely citation errors, flagged below. A `confirmed` status means a public source was located and checked against the claim — it still isn't approval to reuse the figure without review.

Source: [the-digital-nicholas-olsen/marketing-ai](https://github.com/the-digital-nicholas-olsen/marketing-ai/tree/main/assets/claims-reference) — external claims-audit repo, `optilight.md`, reviewed 2026-09-09 (owner: Kaelyn Gray).

Source materials the audit reviewed:

- **Deck** — "OptiLight Pitch Deck, Rev A" (PB-00030660, Rev A), 29-slide practice-facing pitch deck.
- **Trifold** — "OptiLiGHT Patient Trifold B2C" (PB-00032060, Rev C), 2-page consumer-facing piece, copyright 2024.

## High-priority flags

- **OG-4 / OG-5 (likely citation error):** the deck's "81% frustrated" and "80% want something more effective" stats both link to the same source URL, whose own highlighted text fragment reads "72 percent" — not 81% or 80%. Both statistics point to the identical URL and text fragment, suggesting a copy/paste citation that was never updated. Check the source article directly before using either number.
- **OG-1:** the deck ("49 million Americans have DED") and the trifold ("between 30 to 40 million") currently state different figures for the same claim — not a rounding difference. The deck's figure is also derived from population data accessed in 2020.
- **OG-12:** the trifold's "6.3x more expressible glands" figure does not match the headline numbers in its likely source paper, which computes to about 4.7x from that paper's reported values (8.0 vs. 1.7 glands). The "2.7x tear breakup time" figure in the same claim does match the source (2.0 sec vs. 0.75 sec).
- **OG-13:** "the first and only [light therapy/IPL], FDA approved for management of Dry Eye Disease" is a strong, absolute exclusivity claim repeated in both practice and consumer materials, with no source cited for the "first and only" element itself. Regulatory exclusivity claims can go stale as competitors receive new clearances — confirm this is still accurate as of any date this material is redistributed, rather than treating it as a stable, one-time-verified fact.

## Notes and flags

- **High-priority:** OG-4 and OG-5 are the clearest catch in this audit — both point to a URL whose own highlighted text says "72 percent," not the 81%/80% shown on the slide. Should be checked and corrected (or re-sourced) before the deck's next revision.
- **OG-1:** the "Americans with dry eye" figure genuinely differs between the current trifold (30-40M) and the deck (49M) — not rounding of the same number.
- **OG-12:** the 6.3x gland-count figure is the one quantitative claim in this pass that most clearly doesn't reconcile with the identified source paper's headline numbers (4.7x by this review's calculation) — worth a direct check against the paper's full tables before the next reprint.
- Several `needs review` entries (OG-2, OG-3, OG-6, OG-9, OG-10, OG-11) reflect that the cited source was confirmed to exist and be on-topic, but individual numbers weren't re-derived from the primary source's full text/tables — that deeper check is still worth doing before treating those figures as fully verified, especially before new external use.

## How to use this

- Check the Status column before citing any figure externally — treat `needs review` as **not yet usable**, not as a lesser form of confirmed.
- This file audits claims in _current_ materials as of 2026-09-09; it does not replace a fresh regulatory/legal check on new copy, and figures/regulatory status can change between material revisions.
- Cross-reference [SKILL.md](SKILL.md)'s existing market-scale figures (49M Americans with DED, 86% MGD share) against OG-1 and OG-7 above — those SKILL.md figures come from the same unresolved sourcing this file flags.
