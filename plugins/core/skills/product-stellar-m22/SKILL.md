# Stellar M22

Stellar M22 is a multi-application platform (an upgrade from the M22 device, released in 2020) with four handpieces, offering treatments for at least 30 skin conditions — making it adaptable to many different practice profiles.

## Target audience
- Practices wanting a single, versatile device that can be integrated into a broad range of treatment offerings.

## Talking points

- Broad practice fit — from acne to pigment to hair removal to tattoo removal.

## Features
- Stellar IPL: 9 filters addressing acne, vascularity, hair removal, and pigment.
- ResurFX: 1565 nm Er:glass fractional laser.
- Multi-spot Nd:YAG: long pulse, addresses a range of vascular concerns.
- Q-Switch Nd:YAG: 4 disposable tip spot sizes; 6 mm tip for toning; ideal for tattoo removal; melasma treatable with the toning tip.
- Branded protocol example: "Smooth Glow" = IPL + RFMN combination.
