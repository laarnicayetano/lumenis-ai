# Stellar M22

Stellar M22 is Lumenis's flagship multi-application platform — one device, four technologies, thirty FDA-cleared/unique treatments — originally introduced ~2020 and relaunched in 2025 with XPL™ technology. Tagline: "The Expert Tool to Elevate Your Practice." Available in the US and Europe.

## Target audience
- Practices wanting a single, versatile device that can be integrated into a broad range of treatment offerings.
- Dermatologic surgeons and cosmetic dermatology practitioners specifically, in addition to general aesthetic practices.
- Market context: energy-based devices are a $3.4B+ segment of US MedSpa revenue, growing ~20% annually; laser hair removal, non-surgical resurfacing, and IPL are among the most-offered procedures at US practices.

## Talking points
- One platform, four technologies, 30 FDA-cleared/unique treatments (the XPL handpiece alone carries 23 FDA-cleared indications).
- Broad practice fit — from acne to pigment to hair removal to tattoo removal.
- Company/technology heritage: Lumenis invented the original IPL system (1993 patent) — three decades of direct lineage to today's device. Cited proof points: 170+ peer-reviewed clinical studies on XPL and its predecessors, 2,500+ patients tracked for up to 12 years in a single long-term IPL study, 90%+ efficacy rate for reversing photodamage (per a cited 2016 study), 30,000+ installed devices worldwide.
- Practice support positioned as an ongoing relationship, not just a purchase: dedicated on-site clinical training (Lumenis Accelerate program), a service network staffed entirely by full-time Lumenis employees (no third-party/depot repair), and patient-acquisition tools (marketing materials, Lumenis App).
- Named physician endorsements available: Dr. Mitchel Goldman (Cosmetic Laser Dermatology, San Diego — "gold standard of IPLs"), Dr. Michael D. Mirmanesh and Travis J. Laverne, MHS, PA-C (The Garden Medical Spa, Tampa Bay).
- Do not cite specific pricing, per-session cost, or dollar-based ROI figures in external copy — flag to the account/sales team instead if a prospect asks.

## Features
- XPL™ (Expert Pulse Light): broad-spectrum IPL, 8 expert filters (515–1200nm) plus 3 lightguides, MSP™ multi-sub-pulse technology (2–3 precisely spaced sub-pulses per emission for comfort/safety across skin types I–V) and OPT (Optimal Pulse Technology, square/stable top-hat pulses for consistent energy delivery).
- XPL™ Glide Mode: a lower-fluence, up-to-4Hz maintenance setting for routine "prejuvenation" touch-ups, distinct from the standard corrective XPL protocol.
- ResurFX®: non-ablative fractional resurfacing reaching near 1mm depth; SapphireCool™ tip (10°C) with the CoolScan™ algorithm; BrushFX & CoolScan non-sequential pulsing for blending/feathering treatment areas; low social downtime.
- Multi-Spot™ Nd:YAG: large-spot, deep-penetrating laser for vascular lesions, complementary to XPL treatments.
- Q-Switched Nd:YAG: non-thermal, photo-acoustic laser for pigment and dark tattoo removal, skin types I–VI.
