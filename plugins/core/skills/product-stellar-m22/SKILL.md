# Stellar M22

Stellar M22 is a multi-application platform (an upgrade from the M22 device, announced March 2020) with four handpieces, offering treatments for at least 30 skin conditions — making it adaptable to many different practice profiles. Available in the US and Europe.

## Target audience
- Practices wanting a single, versatile device that can be integrated into a broad range of treatment offerings.
- Dermatologic surgeons and cosmetic dermatology practitioners specifically, in addition to general aesthetic practices.

## Talking points
- One platform, four handpieces, 30+ treatable skin conditions.
- Broad practice fit — from acne to pigment to hair removal to tattoo removal.
- Company heritage: Lumenis invented the original IPL system. Cited proof points: 150+ clinical studies, 30,000+ systems sold worldwide, 850+ science-based presets.

## Features
- XPL (Expert Pulse Light) handpiece: 8 expert filters plus 3 lightguides, addressing acne, vascularity, hair removal, and pigment. (Note: this file previously called it "Stellar IPL" with 9 filters — "XPL" and 8 filters is what Lumenis's current site uses; confirm before publishing if this differs from internal spec sheets.)
- ResurFX: 1565 nm Er:glass fractional laser.
- Multi-spot Nd:YAG: long pulse, addresses a range of vascular concerns.
- Q-Switch Nd:YAG: 4 disposable tip spot sizes; 6 mm tip for toning; ideal for tattoo removal; melasma treatable with the toning tip.
- Branded protocol example: "Smooth Glow" = IPL + RFMN combination.
