# About Lumenis

Sourced from lumenis.com. Re-check the source pages periodically — leadership and
figures change.

## Company at a glance

Lumenis develops and commercializes energy-based medical technologies —
laser, intense pulsed light (IPL), and radiofrequency systems — for two
markets: **Aesthetics** and **Vision**. The company positions itself as the
global category leader in energy-based aesthetic and ophthalmologic
solutions, with a heritage of "60 Years Pushing Boundaries" and messaging
built around practitioner success ("Your success is our mission") and
patient-facing "#CelebratingYou" branding.

## What we do

- **Aesthetics:** hair removal, skin rejuvenation, body contouring, hair
  loss treatment, scar revision, tattoo removal, women's health
  applications. Key device lines: FoLix, triLift, Stellar M22, SPLENDOR X,
  Digital Duet.
- **Vision:** dry eye disease management, glaucoma treatment, retinal
  photocoagulation. Key device line: OptiLIGHT.

Lumenis describes itself as a "BeautyTech pioneer," emphasizing innovation
in conditions previously considered untreatable, and backs its positioning
with 500+ clinical publications.

## Mission & values

Stated purpose: "we empower people and innovate relentlessly," aimed at
making a meaningful impact and promoting genuine well-being. Three stated
operating principles:

- Taking ownership
- Evolving and staying ahead
- Aiming high

## Key facts

- ~1,500 employees worldwide
- Active in 100+ countries across four continents (homepage states 88+
  countries; About page states 100+ — About is the more recent/authoritative
  figure)
- 80,000–90,000+ installed systems globally (sources vary slightly)
- 330+ registered patents
- Headquartered in Israel, with international offices
- Portfolio company of Baring Private Equity Asia
- Numerous FDA clearances across its device portfolio
