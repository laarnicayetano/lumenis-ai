# OptiLIFT claims reference

A sourced audit of clinical, regulatory and epidemiological claims/statistics used in current OptiLIFT marketing materials, with citations and a per-claim review status.

**Read before quoting a figure externally.** This is a record of what current materials say and what their own footnotes cite — it is **not** pre-cleared copy and not a substitute for regulatory/medical/legal review of new copy. Most rows below are marked `needs review`: the cited source couldn't be independently confirmed, the number doesn't clearly reconcile with the source, or two current materials give different numbers for the same claim. A `confirmed` status means a public source was located and checked against the claim — it still isn't approval to reuse the figure without review.

Source: [the-digital-nicholas-olsen/marketing-ai](https://github.com/the-digital-nicholas-olsen/marketing-ai/tree/main/assets/claims-reference) — external claims-audit repo, `optilift.md`, reviewed 2026-09-09 (owner: Kaelyn Gray). This is not a lumenis.com source; treat figures as needing a direct check against current regulatory/legal-approved material before external use.

Source materials the audit reviewed:
- **Deck** — "OptiLIFT Product PPT, Rev. A" (PB-00087230, Rev A), 35-slide practice-facing pitch deck.
- **Brochure** — "Brochure B2C Press digital" (PB-00088980, Rev A), 6-page consumer-facing brochure.

## High-priority flags

- **OL-1:** deck and brochure both state muscles "lose 3-8% ... every decade" but cite two different, unrelated sources — a general (non-facial) sarcopenia review, and a dry-eye epidemiology page that doesn't contain the stat.
- **OL-4 / OL-7:** the "2.2-fold risk" figure and the "LLDT > 6mm" threshold could not be located in their cited source (Doan et al. 2020) during this audit.
- **OL-5:** slide 8 has two unsourced, conflicting figures ("$3.98B market in 2023" vs. "7.1% CAGR by 2032") with visibly overlapping slide text, suggesting an incomplete edit.
- **OL-8 (good news):** the deck cites this study as "data on file; manuscript under preparation," but it has since been peer-reviewed and published (Chelnis & Chelnis, *Clin Ophthalmol* 2025) with figures that support the deck's claims. The deck's citation is stale and should be updated to the published reference.

## Notes and flags

- **OL-N1 (3-8%/decade citation conflict):** two materials state the identical figure but cite two unrelated sources. Recommend confirming a single correct source, and consider softening "muscles beneath our eyes" to match what the general-sarcopenia source actually supports, or finding a periorbital-specific source.
- **OL-N2 (2.2-fold risk figure):** reused across OptiLIFT (and OptiLIGHT) materials pointing to the same Doan et al. 2020 paper; could not be confirmed from the paper's retrievable content in this audit. Worth a direct human check of the published PDF/tables.
- **OL-N3 (eyelid surgery market size):** two unsourced, conflicting figures with visibly overlapping slide text. Confirm intent and add a citation, or remove the unsourced figure.
- **OL-N4 (superseded citation):** OL-8's study has moved from "data on file" to peer-reviewed publication since the deck was produced — good for claim strength, but the deck should be revised to cite the published paper, and any prior sales/legal review that treated this as unpublished data should be aware it's now public.

## How to use this

- Check the Status column before citing any figure externally — treat `needs review` as **not yet usable**, not as a lesser form of confirmed.
- This file audits claims in *current* materials as of 2026-09-09; it does not replace a fresh regulatory/legal check on new copy, and figures/regulatory status can change between material revisions.
- Cross-reference [SKILL.md](SKILL.md)'s existing caution on FDA/regulatory status — this file adds sourcing detail but doesn't resolve that open question.
